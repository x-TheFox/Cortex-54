# Privacy Policy

**Version 1 · effective 27 October 2026**

Savant is built to keep your data on your computer. This policy explains the little we do receive, why, and your
choices.

## What stays on your computer

Your settings, API keys (in a file only your user can read, or your system keychain), broker connection, trading
journal, positions, AI model conversations and research all live on your computer. We don't receive them. Savant has
**no analytics or usage telemetry**.

## What we receive

- **Licence checks** (when you activate a licence key, and about once a day while it's active): your licence key, a
  one-way fingerprint of your computer (a hash; the original hardware id never leaves your computer), the app type,
  and your IP address as part of the internet connection. We use this only to check that the key is valid and bound
  to one computer, and to prevent abuse (rate limits). The key's binding is stored with your purchase at Whop; the
  licence server keeps no database of its own, and our hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps short-lived request logs.
- **Purchases** are handled by **Whop**, which processes your payment and account details under its own privacy
  policy. We receive what Whop provides to sellers (for example your Whop username or email, plan, status and
  payment history) and use it to provide the licence, support and refunds.
- **Support**: anything you choose to send us (for example a support file — it contains versions, settings and
  check results, never your keys; you can read it before sending).
- **Community hub (optional, off by default)**: if you choose to publish, your pseudonymous agent name, its public
  key and the trade record you publish are public. If you follow other agents, the hub receives your requests.
  Published records can't be edited, so leaving stops new publishing but keeps what was published; if you ask us to
  delete your agent, we remove its name and records from the hub.

## What third parties receive (because you set them up)

Savant connects from your computer directly to services you configure: your broker (e.g. Alpaca), AI model
providers, Cloudflare Workers AI, ntfy (phone alerts), Discord, market-data sources and websites the research desk
reads. They receive what each connection needs (for example AI providers receive the prompts, which include market
data and news; ntfy receives alert text, which may include positions and P&L). Their own privacy policies apply.
Choose providers you trust; some free AI tiers may use prompts for training.

## Legal bases, retention and your rights

We process the data above to perform our contract with you (licences, support) and for our legitimate interest in
preventing abuse. Licence-check data lives with your Whop purchase for as long as it is active plus what Whop
retains; support messages are kept as long as needed to help you. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK
GDPR, California's CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to
processing. Ask through the Savant support chat on Whop. We don't sell personal data and don't use it for
advertising.

## Children

Savant isn't for anyone under 18.

## Changes

We'll show material changes in the app and on the website before they apply.
