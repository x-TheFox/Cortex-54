I Intake

Thinking costs money. It spends it like money.

Cheap code reads everything. Small models read what is left. The expensive hearing is kept for the few ideas that survive — at most %%T3_PER_DAY%% a day. Every idea that dies gets a written reason.

    Noise, duplicates, stale quotes, spreads, fee hurdles. All arithmetic happens here, in code. At most %%T0_INTAKE%% headlines a day reach the queue.

    One headline per call, because batching distorted the scores. Promoted only at P(material) ≥ %%T1_BAR%% and priority ≥ %%T1_ESC%%; at most %%T1_PER_PULSE%% a pulse.

V When it is wrong

Losing makes it smaller. Losing more makes it stop.

  3. Orange

    %%B_ORANGE%% – %%B_RED%%

    No new entries. Exits only.

  4. Red

    %%B_RED%%+, a %%B_RED_DAILY%% day, or %%B_STOPOUTS%% stop-outs in 24 h

    Sell everything and lock. Real money unlocks only with a written postmortem.

Every position has an exit.

A stop at the broker wherever it accepts one, and a stop checked by the brain on every pulse wherever it doesn't. Stops only ever move up.

It fails closed.

Stale data, an order it doesn't recognise, a position it didn't open, a budget at %%HL_BUDGET%%: new entries stop. A kill flag it can't read counts as killed.

A watchdog with none of its code.

In the cloud edition a separate sentinel, deployed on its own, checks the heartbeat every minute and can sell everything — even if the brain itself is broken. On the desktop the kill switch is in the tray.

VI Graduation

Paper and live, side by side. One is allowed to trade.

Both run the same brain and the same contracts, each sized for its own balance — whatever that balance is. Nobody approves trades; there is no mode for it. Real money starts only when you ratify it, with the paper record in front of you.

PAPER · trading on its own

Paper

Pretend money, real prices. It charges itself modelled fees and slippage that the paper broker doesn't, so the record isn't flattered.

LIVE · dormant at Stop 0

Live

Real money. It joins at Stop 0 and places nothing until a person ratifies it. The gate below is what the app checks for you first.

The graduation gate

Tracked from data by the Governor and shown beside the ratify control:

  • Every roadmap phase built
  • 30 straight days of fully autonomous paper trading
  • 25 or more closed trades
  • Positive expectancy after fees, profit factor above 1.2
  • Zero rail violations
  • Heartbeat at 99.9% or better
  • Calibrated Brier score under 0.25
  • Every red breaker recovered and reviewed
  • A live kill-switch drill that passes

Then a person types RATIFY — and confirms it again, 10 minutes to 24 hours later, on a fresh sign-in.

On your own install you decide: you can connect a live account at any time. It joins dormant at Stop 0, and the ratify screen shows this gate's verdict as a warning, not a block: you decide. Everything else still binds it.
