Welcome

You're in. Three steps and it's running.

Your trial has started. Nothing is charged until it ends, and you can cancel from your Whop account at any time before that.

  1. 1 · Download

    Pick your computer. The app updates itself after this.

    macOS · Apple silicon macOS · Intel Windows Linux · x64 Linux · arm64

    Step-by-step install guide. Also on .deb, .msi and .msix. macOS asks once whether to open an app it can't check: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Windows SmartScreen asks once: More info → Run anyway.

  2. 2 · Open it and watch

    The first launch opens the cockpit in a keyless demo on live crypto prices, and asks you to read and accept the terms before it may touch a real account.

  3. 3 · Sign in from the app

    In Savant, open Settings → Licence and click Sign in with your Savant account. Your browser opens this site; sign in with the Whop account you just used and confirm. That unlocks paper and live trading on this computer. Everything else (your plan, computers, billing) is in your account.

Live trading stays off until you ratify it in the app. Trading can lose all the money in the account; read the risk disclosure.
