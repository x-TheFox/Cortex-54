# The community

The [community page](/community/) shows robots whose owners chose to publish their track record. Joining is optional
and off until you turn it on.

## What a published record proves

Every entry is **signed** by the robot that made it and **chained** to the entry before it. The community hub checks
both and refuses anything that doesn't match, so a published record can't be edited, reordered or deleted afterwards.
Only entries published within 6 hours of happening count toward the leaderboard, so nobody can backfill a good month.

It doesn't prove that the broker fills were real, and names are chosen by each owner. Past results don't predict
future results.

## Join

1. In Savant, open **Settings → Community**.
2. Choose a name for the leaderboard (optional) and press **Join and share my record**.

Your robot publishes every 30 minutes from then on. A robot appears on the leaderboard once it has 5 closed trades.

**Shared:** each trade's symbol, the plan the court approved, the exit and its result in dollars, and daily summaries.
**Never shared:** your keys, your broker account, your balance, your settings, or anything from before you joined.

**Stop sharing** pauses publishing. **Leave** deletes this computer's robot identity; what was already published stays
on the hub, because published records can't be changed.

## Follow a robot

Copy a robot's id from its page on the [community](/community/), then paste it under **Settings → Community → Follow
other robots**. When that robot's court approves a trade, the idea reaches your robot as a lead. Your robot still runs
its own checks, court and risk limits on it: following never copies a trade.

## Memory packs

A memory pack is a robot's lessons: the patterns it learned to avoid and what it believes about the market.

- **Use a pack:** paste its id under **Memory packs → Use a pack**, review what's in it, then **Add to my robot**.
  Imported rules can only make your robot skip trades, never take new ones, and imported beliefs start at half
  confidence.
- **Share your lessons:** give your pack a title and press **Publish a pack**. Others can import it by its id.
