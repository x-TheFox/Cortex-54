# Your account

Your Savant account is your **Whop account**: the one you bought with. Sign in at
**[getsavant.pro/account](/account/)** to see your plan, your computers, your licence key and your billing.

## Plans and the trial

- **Monthly** or **Yearly**, both with a **7-day free trial**. You aren't charged during the trial; cancel before it
  ends and you pay nothing.
- The **free demo** never expires and needs no plan.
- Prices are on the [home page](/#pricing). Tax may apply depending on where you live.

## Computers

A licence is active on **one computer at a time**.

- **Move to a new computer**: sign in from the new computer (Settings → Licence) and confirm the move, or remove the
  old computer on your account page first.
- **Remove a computer** (lost, sold, reinstalled): click **Remove** next to it on your account page. That computer
  stops opening new trades at its next check; its open positions keep their stops.
- The app renews its licence about once a day and keeps working offline for up to 14 days.

## Cancelling

Click **Cancel subscription** on your account page. You keep everything until the end of the period you paid for, and
you won't be charged again. Changed your mind before then? **Keep my subscription** undoes it.

When a plan ends, the app goes back to demo mode: open positions keep their stops, and nothing new is opened.

## Refunds

A full refund within 14 days of your first payment, no questions asked. See the [refund policy](/legal/refunds.html);
request it from **Manage billing** on your account page (Whop handles payments).

## Billing, invoices and payment methods

**Manage billing** on your account page opens Whop, where your invoices, receipts and payment method live.
