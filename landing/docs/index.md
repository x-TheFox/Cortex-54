# Savant docs

Savant runs on your own computer and trades your own Alpaca account. These pages take you from download to a running
robot, and cover your account, live trading and what to do when something looks wrong.

- **[Install Savant](install/)** on macOS, Windows or Linux, including the one-time security prompts.
- **[Getting started](getting-started/)**: first launch, signing in, your paper account, AI models, alerts.
- **[Your account](account/)**: plans, the free trial, moving to another computer, cancelling, refunds.
- **[Live trading](live-trading/)**: connecting a real-money account and switching it on.
- **[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting/)**: the common problems and their fixes.
- **[Uninstall](uninstall/)**: removing the app and everything it keeps.

Questions the docs don't answer: open **Support** in your [Whop orders](https://whop.com/@me/settings/orders) and write
to us there.

> Trading can lose all of the money in the account. Savant is software, not financial advice. Read the
> [risk disclosure](/legal/risk.html) before you connect a broker account.
