# Troubleshooting

**The app says "Savant can't start: its files don't match this installation."** The app's files were changed or
damaged. Download Savant again and reinstall; your settings and journal are kept.

**macOS: "can't check the app for malicious software" or "damaged".** See [Install → macOS](../install/#macos).

**Windows: "Windows protected your PC".** Click **More info → Run anyway** ([Install → Windows](../install/#windows)).

**"Licence: … No new trades."** Open **Settings → Licence**. If your plan is active, sign in again. If the licence
was moved to another computer, move it back from [your account](/account/). The demo always works.

**"This key is active on another computer."** A licence is on one computer at a time. Remove the old computer on your
account page, or confirm the move when you sign in on the new one.

**Nothing is trading.** Usually by design: the robot only trades when an idea survives every check. Look at the
cockpit's memo line and **FUNNEL** page: they say why. Amber or red lights name what's blocking (stale data, a budget,
a breaker, a missing model).

**"The court needs AI models".** Add at least two models from different families in **Settings → AI models**.

**Research, forecasts and the weekly review don't run.** Install uv ([Install → research desk](../install/#research))
and restart Savant.

**Phone can't connect.** Phone access works on the same Wi-Fi only; turn it on in **Settings → Phone** and scan the QR
code again.

## Getting help

**Settings → Health → Support file** saves a report with no keys or account numbers in it. Send it to us from
**Support** in your [Whop orders](https://whop.com/@me/settings/orders).
